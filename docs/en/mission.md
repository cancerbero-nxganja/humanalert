# HumanAlert — Mission Manifesto

## Three Truths

**We only have each other as a species.**  
In a crisis — earthquake, flood, conflict, pandemic — the first responder is always a neighbor. HumanAlert puts the tools of coordination in every pocket, so humans can reach other humans before institutional help arrives.

**The planet is our home.**  
We build with the assumption that infrastructure can fail. Every feature works on 2G networks. Every screen works without connectivity. We choose OpenStreetMap because the map of the world belongs to the world.

**Animals are free beings.**  
Lost pets, displaced wildlife, animals in distress: they cannot call for help. HumanAlert integrates with the PetWhisper / AlertaMascota ecosystem to give them a voice. This integration is free, always, with no exceptions.

---

## What We Build

HumanAlert is a humanitarian emergency coordination platform. It lets institutions, communities, and individuals:

- Issue and receive real-time alerts (emergencies, weather, health, evacuation)
- Report and search for missing persons with AMBER Alert support
- Report lost and found animals in cooperation with PetWhisper
- Share community resource pins (shelters, food, water, medical aid)

Everything works offline. Everything respects privacy. No advertising. No data monetization.

---

## How We Work

**Open source, forever.** The entire codebase is public on GitHub under a free and open license.

**100% donated.** All revenue from HumanAlert — donations, grants, institutional contracts — goes to humanitarian causes. The builders volunteer their time.

**Quality without compromise.** We maintain 70%+ test coverage, WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility, and support 8 languages including right-to-left Arabic. A system used in crisis must never fail because of a bug, a broken screen reader, or an untranslated message.

**Privacy by design.** We never store last names (initial only), never store photos (hash references only), and never require personal data to receive an alert or report a missing person.
